description: "Thoughts on mental illness, stigma, people, and relationships."
date: 2026-11-20

My people,

The abyss has taken over me. As pristine as your soul is, I am afraid the abyss may stealth into you.

Your life is sweet, as the abyss may turn the sweet to bitter. I want you to be gone far away from me!

You have filled my void with light. As it becomes dark, from afar I will look into you, as the sheer sight of you may scare the abyss.

By the time the odour of the abyss becomes the stench of a corpus, sit beside me and cry for me, as if your presence could fill me with light.

Guy with a moustache - on random night of November
