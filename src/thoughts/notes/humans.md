My experience so far with humans has been disappointing. I regret being born into this species. I would rather be a donkey than a human.
All humans are evil.

I tried my best to understand humans and connections. I was told the connections would fix me. Living with CPTSD has been hard.
For me, humans are evil. They loved me then abandoned me; they abused me then loved me. Isn't this all a space that was meant to hurt me?
Should I continue among these evils? I wish to be far away from humans, completely isolated from the evilness of humans.

I have been benevolent enough to explore humans and their connection, believing it could fix me; it's turned to the latter. It hurts more. I am sorry I failed to understand humans and connections. For the few people whom I managed to understand, I am really sorry I don't have much choice left. For the rest who put me in the middle, I wish I had never met you in my life. I regret everything.

I no longer wish for a cure for my mental illness; I wish to erase all the people I come across in my life, even my parents.
I only wish to remember my grandma and my little sisters. The rest I need to be gone and erased from me. But unfortunately, such absurd ideas are not possible.

I regret I need to come in contact with humans, talk with them. I regret meeting humans, being friends with them, talking to them, and everything. I was a believer in God; he told me to love all, and I thought people would love me back. It never happened. Now I am happily seeking refuge with evil. As God comes or reveals someday, I want to confront him.

The thing about humans is that they are vile, cruel, and evil. What kind of absurdity was I born into?
They tell you it was never my mistake and act like it was.

I am flawed, and I am so frustrated about not being dead.
I wish to be soon; I don't want to be human, even close to their feces.
