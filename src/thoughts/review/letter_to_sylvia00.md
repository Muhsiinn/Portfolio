Ms.Sarah is really understanding of me, she is sending me for a specilised treatment in semester break.  

I wish I was with you before you leave. when we meet next time we can  talk a lot. I bet you have a lot to say. I am little sad that you went a little early. You should've have a session with Ms.Sarah. Well, next time. I was wondering why the omnipotent loving God made us this way. I wish things were different.
